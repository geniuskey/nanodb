## Why it failed

Double rounding, not a coding error in the generated program.

Stored `value_nm` for the two Thickness measurements are `11.331` and `11.01625`.
Their mean is `11.173625`, which displays half-up as **11.17** — the expected
value in `checks.json`. The operator pasted the *displayed* values `11.33` and
`11.02`; their mean is `11.175`, which displays half-up as **11.18**.

CD and Depth happened to survive the same transcription, so the fault is not
uniform — it appears only where the discarded digits push the mean across a
rounding boundary. That is what makes it easy to miss by eye.
